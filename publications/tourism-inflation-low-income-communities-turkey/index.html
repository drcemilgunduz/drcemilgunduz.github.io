Tourism, inflation and low-income communities in Turkey: impacts on domestic travel behaviour and local resilience

Cemil Gündüz, Mojtaba Rezaei, Onur Atak, Ciro Troise, Stefano Bresciani · 2025

Abstract

Examines how rising inflation in Turkey has reshaped domestic travel behaviour among low-income households, and what this means for the resilience of local tourism economies.
