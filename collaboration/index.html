Research Collaboration

Open to collaboration with researchers, research groups and institutions working at the intersection of tourism, artificial intelligence, digital transformation and sustainability.

Current Network

Cemil Gündüz has conducted collaborative research with a wide range of international and national researchers, including Mojtaba Rezaei, Marco Pironti, Davide Calandra, Paolo Biancone, Stefano Bresciani, Roberto Quaglia, Ciro Troise, Nizar Ghamgui, Tomas Kliestik, Cafer Topaloğlu, Hüseyin Çeken, Umut Avcı, Levent Karadağ, Yılmaz Ağca, Onur Atak, Şeyhmus Demircan and Taner Dalgın, resulting in co-authored publications across leading international journals. He also continues to work with colleagues in the COST Action CRICULT network on transdisciplinary cultural heritage research.

Areas Open to Collaboration

Artificial Intelligence & Digital Transformation in Tourism

Comparative studies, AI ethics in tourism and hospitality, and applications of digital technologies in destination management.

Sustainable Tourism

Environmental certification, destination resilience, and sustainability practices in hospitality.

Gastronomy & Cultural Tourism

Cross-country comparative research on culinary tourism, festivals and cultural diplomacy.

Cultural Heritage

Through the COST Action CRICULT network, on legal, regulatory and transdisciplinary approaches to cultural heritage in crisis.
