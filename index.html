About

Cemil Gündüz is an Associate Professor at Tokat Gaziosmanpaşa University, Niksar Vocational School, where he also serves as Head of Department. His current work focuses on artificial intelligence, digital transformation and sustainable tourism — an area he has actively pursued through recent publications, conference contributions, and specialised training in AI applications and ethics. This direction follows a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Turin (2022–2023) and an earlier foundation in tourism competitive strategy, including his doctoral research.
