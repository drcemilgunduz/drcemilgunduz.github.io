Gastronomy Tourism & Cultural Diplomacy

Research Question

How does food-related tourism function as a form of cultural exchange and diplomacy between countries?

Why It Matters

Gastronomy festivals and culinary heritage increasingly serve as vehicles for cross-cultural understanding and destination branding.

Contribution

This theme is anchored by a comparative study of gastronomy festivals in Turkey and Italy, published in British Food Journal, and a book chapter on the digitalisation of local culinary culture.
