Current Focus

Sustainable Tourism

Research Question

How can tourism destinations and businesses balance environmental responsibility with economic and social development?

Why It Matters

Environmental certification, resource management and community resilience are increasingly central to tourism policy and business practice.

Contribution

Research in this area includes an evaluation of environmental practices in Green Key certified hotels in Turkey, and a qualitative study of the environmental effects of tourism activity in the Niksar Çamiçi Plateau.
