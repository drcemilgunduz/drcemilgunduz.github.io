Current Focus

Artificial Intelligence & Digital Transformation in Tourism

Research Question

How are artificial intelligence and digital technologies reshaping decision-making, service delivery and knowledge management in tourism and hospitality?

Why It Matters

Tourism and hospitality businesses are increasingly adopting AI-driven tools and digital platforms, raising practical and ethical questions about how these technologies are used responsibly and effectively.

Contribution

Cemil Gündüz's contribution to this area includes conference research on the ethical challenges of AI adoption in knowledge management, applied case studies on AI-driven personalisation in aviation and hospitality, and book chapters examining digital transformation and smart destination management. He has also completed specialised training in AI ethics and applications relevant to this direction.

This is a developing research direction, actively pursued through recent publications, conference contributions and continued professional training.
