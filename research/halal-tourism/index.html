Doctoral Background

Halal Tourism & Competitive Strategy

Research Question

What strategic and competitive factors shape the halal tourism and hospitality market, and how is this market structured across different countries?

Why It Matters

Halal tourism represents a significant and growing segment of the global tourism market, with distinct certification, marketing and operational requirements.

Contribution

This was Cemil Gündüz's doctoral research area (Muğla Sıtkı Koçman University, 2019) and the subject of a dedicated book, several journal articles, and a web content analysis of the halal tourism market in Italy conducted during his time at the University of Turin.

An established area of expertise from earlier doctoral and postdoctoral work.
