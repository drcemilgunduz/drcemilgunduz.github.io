International Experience

A postdoctoral fellowship at the University of Turin and continued participation in European research and mobility programmes connect this work to international academic networks. Over the course of his academic career, Cemil Gündüz has presented research, undertaken training, and participated in academic and Erasmus+ activities across several European countries, including Italy, Hungary, Lithuania, Portugal, Spain and the Czech Republic.
